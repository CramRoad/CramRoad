Privacy Policy

Short version: this website collects nothing. There are no accounts, no tracking and no servers — everything runs in your own browser.

Last updated: September 2026

1. No data collection

CramRoad Study does not ask you to sign up, log in, or share any personal information. We do not run analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies. Simply browsing chapters sends no personal data to anyone, because there is no backend server to send it to.

2. Search stays on your device

The chapter search box filters a built-in list using JavaScript inside your browser. Your search terms never leave your device.

3. Contact form

If you use the Contact page, your message opens in your own email app and is sent as a normal email. Only the information you choose to write is shared, and only with us.

4. External hosting

  • If this site is hosted on GitHub Pages (or a similar static host), that provider may log basic technical data such as IP addresses under its own privacy policy.
  • Tutorial pages do not embed third-party videos, ads, or social-media widgets.

5. Children

This site is suitable for students of all ages. Since we collect no data, there is nothing for parents or guardians to review — but we encourage young learners to study with adult guidance.

6. Changes & questions

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new date. Questions? Reach us via the Contact page.
